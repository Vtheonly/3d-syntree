# 02. Study Tracks and Curriculum

This note provides suggested study paths through the 3D-SynTree vault for learners with different backgrounds and time constraints. All tracks follow the prerequisite graph defined in [[01_Prerequisite_Knowledge_Graph]].

## Track A: Absolute Beginner (No Chemistry or Biology Background)

**Estimated time:** 80–120 hours  
**Starting point:** [[01_01_Atomic_Structure_and_Electrons]]  
**Target:** Complete understanding of 3D-SynTree from first principles

### Phase 1: Chemistry Foundations (Weeks 1–3)

Study all notes in [[01_CHEMISTRY_FOUNDATIONS/]] in order:

1. [[01_01_Atomic_Structure_and_Electrons]] — Atoms, protons, neutrons, electrons, orbitals, octet rule
2. [[01_02_Chemical_Bonds_and_Electronegativity]] — Covalent and ionic bonds, polarity, partial charges
3. [[01_03_Molecular_Geometry_and_Hybridization]] — sp³, sp², sp hybridization, VSEPR, bond angles
4. [[01_04_Organic_Functional_Groups]] — Carboxylic acids, amines, amides, alcohols, ethers, halides
5. [[01_05_Resistance_and_Conjugation|01.05 Resonance and Conjugation]] — Amide planarity, electron delocalization
6. [[01_06_Acids_Bases_and_pH74_Ionization]] — pH 7.4, Henderson-Hasselbalch, physiological ionization

### Phase 2: Medicinal Chemistry and Synthesis (Weeks 4–5)

Study all notes in [[02_MEDICINAL_CHEMISTRY_AND_SYNTHESIS/]] in order:

1. [[02_01_Building_Blocks_and_Synthons]] — Purchasable building blocks, theoretical synthons
2. [[02_02_The_Certified_Reaction_Grammar]] — 10 high-yield reaction classes
3. [[02_03_Reaction_SMARTS_and_Isotope_Provenance]] — SMARTS syntax, atom tracking across reactions
4. [[02_04_Chemoselectivity_and_Reactivity_Tiers]] — Reactivity hierarchies, competing handles
5. [[02_05_Fsp3_Saturation_and_Flat_Grease]] — Fsp³ metric, escaping flatland
6. [[02_06_Enamine_REAL_Space_and_Catalogs]] — The 38-billion compound virtual library

### Phase 3: Spatial Chemistry and Force Fields (Weeks 6–7)

Study all notes in [[03_SPATIAL_CHEMISTRY_AND_FORCE_FIELDS/]] in order:

1. [[03_01_Stereochemistry_Chirality_and_CIP_Rules]] — Chiral centers, R/S configuration
2. [[03_02_Rotatable_Bonds_and_Dihedral_Angles]] — SO(2), torsion angles, rotamer basins
3. [[03_03_Van_der_Waals_Radii_and_Steric_Clash]] — Bondi radii, steric overlap
4. [[03_04_Lennard_Jones_and_Soft_Core_Potentials]] — 6-12 potential, soft-core approximation
5. [[03_05_Distance_Geometry_and_ETKDG]] — Conformer generation from 2D graphs
6. [[03_06_Molecular_Mechanics_and_MMFF94_Restraints]] — Force field relaxation, scaffold restraints
7. [[03_07_The_Kabsch_Algorithm_and_Rigid_Alignment]] — SVD-based coordinate alignment

### Phase 4: Structural Biology (Weeks 8–9)

Study all notes in [[04_STRUCTURAL_BIOLOGY_AND_PROTEINS/]] in order:

1. [[04_01_Amino_Acids_and_Peptide_Backbones]] — 20 amino acids, peptide bonds
2. [[04_02_Protein_3D_Hierarchy_and_Folding]] — Primary, secondary, tertiary, quaternary structure
3. [[04_03_Binding_Pockets_and_Cavity_Extraction]] — 10 Å pocket extraction, centering
4. [[04_04_Pharmacophores_and_Interaction_Hotspots]] — Hotspots, salt bridges, H-bonds
5. [[04_05_X-Ray_Crystallography_and_PDB_Artifacts]] — Resolution, B-factors, crystallization artifacts
6. [[04_06_Homology_MMseqs2_Clustering_and_Data_Leakage]] — Sequence clustering, train/test splits

### Phase 5: Pharmacology and Drug Discovery (Weeks 10–11)

Study all notes in [[05_PHARMACOLOGY_AND_DRUG_DISCOVERY/]] in order:

1. [[05_01_Protein_Ligand_Binding_Thermodynamics]] — ΔG, ΔH, ΔS, binding free energy
2. [[05_02_Kinetic_and_Affinity_Metrics_IC50_Kd_Ki]] — Kd, Ki, IC50, potency metrics
3. [[05_03_Drug_Likeness_Lipinski_QED]] — Rule of 5, Veber's rules, QED
4. [[05_04_ADMET_Pharmacokinetics_and_Metabolic_Stability]] — Absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, toxicity
5. [[05_05_Structure_Based_vs_Ligand_Based_Drug_Design]] — SBDD vs LBDD paradigms

### Phase 6: Cheminformatics and Algorithms (Week 12)

Study all notes in [[06_CHEMINFORMATICS_AND_ALGORITHMS/]] in order:

1. [[06_01_SMILES_and_Canonical_Molecular_Graphs]] — SMILES notation, canonicalization
2. [[06_02_Morgan_Circular_Fingerprints]] — ECFP4, bit-vector representations
3. [[06_03_Tanimoto_Similarity_and_Diversity]] — Tanimoto coefficient, molecular diversity
4. [[06_04_Johnson_Lindenstrauss_and_QR_Projections]] — Dimensionality preservation, orthonormal projections
5. [[06_05_Pharm3D_3D_Pharmacophore_Descriptors]] — 2138-dimensional feature vector
6. [[06_06_Hashed_Inverted_Indices_for_Synthon_Retrieval]] — Hash-based candidate retrieval

### Phase 7: Geometric Deep Learning (Weeks 13–14)

Study all notes in [[07_GEOMETRIC_DEEP_LEARNING_AND_EQUIVARIANCE/]] in order:

1. [[07_01_Group_Theory_E3_SE3_SO3_and_SO2]] — E(3), SE(3), SO(3), SO(2) groups
2. [[07_02_Invariance_vs_Equivariance_in_Molecular_Clouds]] — Invariant scalars, equivariant vectors
3. [[07_03_Radial_Basis_Functions_and_Cosine_Cutoffs]] — Gaussian expansion, cutoff envelope
4. [[07_04_PaiNN_Polarizable_Interaction_Networks]] — Scalar/vector channels, interaction blocks
5. [[07_05_Bipartite_Heterogeneous_Protein_Ligand_Graphs]] — Joint protein-ligand graph
6. [[07_06_Cross_Attention_Decoders_for_SBDD]] — Cross-attention mechanism, pocket context

### Phase 8: Circular Statistics and Torsion Heads (Week 15)

Study all notes in [[08_CIRCULAR_STATISTICS_AND_TORSION_HEADS/]] in order:

1. [[08_01_Statistics_on_Riemannian_Manifolds_SO2]] — Circular statistics, periodic domains
2. [[08_02_The_von_Mises_Distribution]] — Circular analogue of Gaussian, κ concentration
3. [[08_03_Modified_Bessel_Functions_and_Amos_Ratio]] — Bessel I₀, autograd stability
4. [[08_04_Categorical_Binned_vs_Continuous_Torsion_Space]] — 36-bin discrete torsion
5. [[08_05_Sub_Bin_Pocket_Conditioned_Dihedral_Refinement]] — Local geometric optimization

### Phase 9: Markov Decision Processes and GFlowNets (Weeks 16–17)

Study all notes in [[09_MARKOV_DECISION_PROCESSES_AND_GFLOWNETS/]] in order:

1. [[09_01_The_Assembly_MDP_Formulation]] — MDP states, actions, transitions
2. [[09_02_Derivation_Trees_vs_Molecular_DAGs]] — Derivation trees vs construction DAGs
3. [[09_03_Trajectory_Balance_GFlowNets_Theory]] — Trajectory Balance loss, partition function
4. [[09_04_Multi_Path_Probability_and_Backward_Flows]] — Backward policy, multi-parent DAGs
5. [[09_05_Stationary_Terminal_Reward_Formulation]] — Stationary composite reward
6. [[09_06_Curriculum_Learning_Approach_C_Quantiles]] — Quantile-based curriculum learning

### Phase 10: Benchmarking and Wet-Lab Translation (Weeks 18–19)

Study all notes in [[10_BENCHMARKING_AND_WET_LAB_TRANSLATION/]] in order:

1. [[10_01_PoseBusters_Physical_Sanity_Filters]] — Physical validity screening
2. [[10_02_AiZynthFinder_Retrosynthetic_Solvability]] — Independent retrosynthetic verification
3. [[10_03_Baseline_Comparisons_TargetDiff_SyntheMol]] — Comparative SBDD baselines
