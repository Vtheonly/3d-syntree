# 01. Prerequisite Knowledge Graph

This note defines the **non-negotiable sequential learning order** for the 3D-SynTree vault. Every node requires mastering the concepts to its left and above before proceeding. Do not skip ahead — advanced topics in geometric deep learning, GFlowNets, and trajectory balance depend on foundations in atomic structure, chemical bonding, protein folding, and thermodynamics.
