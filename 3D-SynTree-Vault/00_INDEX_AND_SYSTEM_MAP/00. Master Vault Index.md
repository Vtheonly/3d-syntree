## How to Use This Vault

1. **Start with the prerequisite graph.** Read [[01_Prerequisite_Knowledge_Graph]] to understand the non-negotiable learning order. Do not skip ahead to GFlowNets or PaiNN before mastering atomic structure, chemical bonding, hybridization, and protein folding.

2. **Follow the concept schema.** Every note follows the strict specification framework defined in [[01_Concept_Specification_Framework]]. Each note contains:
   - What It Is — formal definition from absolute zero
   - Why It Exists — the problem it solves
   - How It Works — mechanisms, derivations, rules
   - What It Consists Of — components, vocabulary, equations
   - What You Must Understand — non-negotiable knowledge and pitfalls
   - Prerequisites — wikilinks to foundational notes
   - Project Connection — direct mapping to 3D-SynTree code, files, tensors
   - Required Depth — Essential | Supporting | Advanced
   - Further Dependencies — concepts that depend on this note

3. **Trace the code.** For every note, open the corresponding file cited in the **Project Connection** section. The 3D-SynTree codebase lives in the adjacent `syntree/` package. Key files include:
   - `syntree/chemistry/reactions.py` — SMARTS reaction templates and atom provenance
   - `syntree/chemistry/conformer.py` — 3D conformer construction, dihedral rotation, clash scoring
   - `syntree/models/torsion_head.py` — Continuous SO(2) torsion head with von Mises distribution
   - `syntree/models/bipartite.py` — Heterogeneous SE(3)-equivariant BipartitePaiNN encoder
   - `syntree/models/policy.py` — End-to-end SynTreePolicy network
   - `syntree/engine/environment.py` — MolecularAssemblyEnv MDP
   - `syntree/engine/rl.py` — ThreeDReward terminal reward function
   - `syntree/data/featurizer.py` — Atomic featurization for pockets, ligands, handles

4. **Use Obsidian features.** This vault makes extensive use of:
   - **Wikilinks** (`[[Note Name]]`) — creates the dense knowledge graph
   - **Tags** — for categorization and discovery
   - **Mermaid mind maps** — for visualizing knowledge hierarchies
   - **Folder hierarchy** — ten modules with recursive subfolders

## The Scientific Problems 3D-SynTree Solves

3D-SynTree addresses three fundamental challenges in AI-driven drug discovery:

### 1. The Synthesizability Wall

Continuous 3D diffusion models generate impossible molecular chimeras (pentavalent carbons, unstable peroxides, extreme angle strain) with less than 20% retrosynthetic feasibility. 3D-SynTree enforces **forward synthetic validity** by assembling building blocks (synthons) from a purchasable catalog (Enamine REAL) via certified medicinal chemistry reactions. Every generated molecule has a verified, actionable synthetic route.

### 2. The 3D Conformational Match

2D forward-synthesis trees (e.g., SynNet, SyntheMol) design synthesizable molecules but operate blind to the 3D binding cavity. 3D-SynTree predicts the exact dihedral angle around newly formed single bonds, relaxing junction bonds via harmonic scaffold restraints in the protein's electrostatic field.

### 3. The Flat Grease Trap

Reaction models default to unconstrained bi-aryl couplings (e.g., Suzuki), generating flat, lipophilic compounds with poor clinical pharmacokinetics. 3D-SynTree imposes an $Fsp^3 \ge 0.40$ constraint to force 3D saturation and drug-likeness.
