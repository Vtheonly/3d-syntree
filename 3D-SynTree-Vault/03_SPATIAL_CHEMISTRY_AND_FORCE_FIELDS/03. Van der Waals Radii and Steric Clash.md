# 03. Van der Waals Radii and Steric Clash

### 1. What It Is

The **van der Waals radius** is the effective volume occupied by an atom's electron cloud, modeled as an impenetrable sphere with a characteristic radius (r_vdw). Atoms cannot approach each other closer than the sum of their van der Waals radii without experiencing strong repulsive forces.

A **steric clash** occurs when two non-bonded atoms are forced closer than their combined van der Waals radii (typically with a small tolerance). This creates a massive energy penalty because the Pauli exclusion principle forbids electron clouds of non-bonded atoms from occupying the same space.

### 2. Why It Exists

The Pauli exclusion principle is a fundamental law of quantum mechanics: two electrons cannot occupy the same quantum state. When electron clouds of adjacent atoms overlap, the electrons must occupy higher-energy states, creating a steeply rising repulsive potential.

This matters for 3D-SynTree because:

- **Steric clashes destroy binding affinity.** If a ligand atom overlaps with a protein atom, the repulsive energy is so large that binding becomes impossible.
- **The model must avoid clashes during generation.** The torsion head and refinement steps must place new fragments in regions of the pocket that are physically accessible.
- **Clash evaluation is part of the reward function.** The soft-core Lennard-Jones potential penalizes overlaps and rewards close-but-not-overlapping contacts.

#### Steric Clash Threshold

Two non-bonded atoms i and j are considered to be in steric clash when:

$$d_{ij} < r_i + r_j - 0.5 \text{ Å}$$

The 0.5 Å tolerance accounts for the fact that atoms can approach slightly closer than the sum of Bondi radii without catastrophic repulsion (the radii are "hard sphere" approximations of soft electron clouds).

#### Clash Loss Function

3D-SynTree computes a clash loss using a soft-core formulation:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{clash}} = \sum_{i \in \text{lig}} \sum_{j \in \text{poc}} \max\left(0, (r_i + r_j - 1.0) - d_{ij}\right)^2$$

This penalizes overlaps quadratically beyond a 1.0 Å switch distance, with zero penalty for non-clashing pairs.

### 4. What It Consists Of

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| van der Waals radius (r_vdw) | Effective atomic radius from electron cloud |
| Bondi radii | Empirical radii table for common elements |
| Steric clash | Overlap of non-bonded atoms below combined radii |
| Pauli exclusion | Quantum principle preventing electron cloud overlap |
| Clash tolerance | Small allowance (0.5 Å) below sum of radii |
| Clash loss | Quadratic penalty for atomic overlaps |
| Non-bonded pair | Atoms not connected by a covalent bond |

### 5. What You Must Understand

- **Van der Waals radii are not hard boundaries.** Electron clouds are soft and fuzzy. Atoms can approach closer than the sum of Bondi radii with gradually increasing energy. The clash tolerance (0.5 Å) reflects this softness.

- **Only non-bonded pairs clash.** Two atoms connected by a covalent bond are expected to be closer than their van der Waals sum—that's what a bond is. The clash check excludes bonded pairs (typically 1-2, 1-3, and sometimes 1-4 relationships).

- **Clash with protein is catastrophic.** A ligand atom inside a protein residue's van der Waals radius creates a repulsive force that can distort the protein or eject the ligand. Binding affinity drops to essentially zero.

- **Common pitfall:** Using Bondi radii without tolerance. This creates false clashes for atoms that are legitimately close (e.g., hydrogen bonding distances where O···O is ~2.8 Å, less than 1.52 + 1.52 = 3.04 Å).

- **Common pitfall:** Forgetting that hydrogens are usually implicit in PDB structures. Clash calculations must account for implicit hydrogen positions or use heavy-atom-only approximations.

### 7. Project Connection

The van der Waals and steric clash concepts map directly to the 3D-SynTree codebase:

- **`VDW_RADII` dictionary** in `syntree/chemistry/conformer.py`: Bondi radii table with DEFAULT_VDW_RADIUS = 1.70 (carbon) fallback for unknown elements.

- **`vdw_radius()`** function in `syntree/chemistry/conformer.py`: Returns Bondi radius for an atomic number, with carbon fallback.

- **`compute_steric_clash_loss()`** in `syntree/chemistry/conformer.py`: Computes the clash loss between ligand and pocket atoms using the formula above.

- **Contact energy reward** in `syntree/engine/rl.py`: Evaluates Lennard-Jones contact energy as part of the ThreeDReward, combining clash penalty with attractive dispersion.

### 8. Required Depth

**Essential.** Steric clash is the primary physical constraint on 3D molecular generation—without it, the model would place atoms inside protein atoms.
